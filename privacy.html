Governance & Compliance // Effective 2026

Privacy & Data Sovereignty Policy

Sons of Harrison (sonsofharrison.com) is committed to strict data minimalism, client confidentiality, and transparent digital governance across our holding company portal and subsidiary links.

SECTION 01 // ZERO TRACKING & LOCAL PREFERENCE STORAGE

Client-Side Interface Preferences

This web portal does not deploy invasive third-party advertising trackers or sell visitor telemetry. To provide a seamless executive browsing experience, your browser's local storage (localStorage) is used strictly to remember three non-identifiable interface preferences:

  • harrison-theme: Stores your selection between Obsidian Dark Mode and Alabaster Light Mode.
  • harrison-crest-finish: Stores your selection for the 9:16 Coat of Arms finish (Forged Steel, Obsidian Dark, or Alabaster Light).
  • harrison-view-mode: Stores your toggle state between Standard Desktop View and the Mobile Center-Scroll Simulator.
SECTION 02 // EXECUTIVE COMMUNICATIONS

Direct Inquiry Confidentiality

When you initiate an inquiry via info@sonsofharrison.com or telephone (443-991-9896), your contact details and project specifications are used exclusively by Sons of Harrison leadership and the designated operating subsidiary (Harrison Hardscaping LLC, Harrison Arbitrage LLC, Harrison Collectibles, or Harrison AI Agents) to respond to your request. We never broker, rent, or disclose client communications to outside marketers.

SECTION 03 // STRUCTURED DATA & AI CRAWLER GOVERNANCE

Legitimate Schema & Intellectual Property
