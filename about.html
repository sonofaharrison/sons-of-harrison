Heraldic Architecture

Anatomy of the Coat of Arms

Every sculpted element within the Sons of Harrison emblem carries deliberate governance meaning across our operating companies.

ELEMENT 01 // LEFT & RIGHT SUPPORTERS

The Trio of Gold Lions & Silver Guardian

Flanking the left side of the shield are three overlapping, sculpted polished-gold rampant lions representing unified forward momentum, offensive market leadership, and brotherhood. Standing guard on the right is a singular, powerful brushed-silver rampant lion clasping the green oak branch—symbolizing vigilant stewardship, structural defense, and wisdom.

ELEMENT 02 // CENTRAL ESCUTCHEON

The Beveled Sky-Blue Enamel Shield

At the heart of the emblem rests a deep, beveled sky-blue enamel shield bearing three embossed golden lions in profile. The azure field denotes sovereign clarity, truth, and fidelity, serving as the immovable core that unites our physical and digital enterprises under one roof.

ELEMENT 03 // SOVEREIGN HELM & CREST

The Bronze-and-Steel Knight's Helm

Crowning the shield is a visor-down bronze-and-steel knight's helm topped by a sovereign coronet and a smaller silver lion bearing an oak branch. The helm represents strategic discipline, executive focus, and protection of proprietary intellectual and physical capital.

ELEMENT 04 // INSCRIPTION & FOUNDATION

The Extruded Gold & Silver Banner

Anchored beneath the shield is crisp white 3D lettering reading "Sons of" above bold, champagne-gold Old English blackletter typography reading "Harrison" with a sharp silver bevel outline—signifying that every subsidiary carries our family name as an explicit guarantee of excellence.
